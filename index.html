Reading Logic - Dashboard 3

The build is sequential. Secondary tells move first, the dollar moves second, the ten-year moves third as foreign duration physically clears back into the long end. By the time the falsifier on the ten-year fires, the underlying move has already happened, which means waiting for the falsifier is a poor risk management strategy. The secondary tells are the leading edge, the primary variables are the trade-management trigger, and the falsifier is only the hard stop.

The Two Clocks

The political clock ends 3 November 2026 with the midterms. The operational clock ends earlier, roughly 7 September, when the autumn issuance calendar starts and the long-end auctions have to clear at survivable yields. Four months is the window we are actually trading - just long enough for Brent to fall back toward $70, USDJPY to head toward 140, and USDCNY to head toward 6.80.

The Single Piece Of Advice

Ignore the CPI prints. They will be hot through June and probably into July because the energy spike is still feeding through services on a lag. The CPI is a distraction. The dials are what matter, and the Friday tape will tell you whether the build is clearing. The single biggest risk is the bond market reading a hot CPI print and pricing a Fed reversal back into the curve before the build has had a chance to clear - which is exactly what Warsh's framework work is designed to insulate against.
